Shipping & Delivery

We dispatch every order within 1–2 business days of confirmation. Standard delivery across India takes 5–8 business days; made-to-order bridal pieces are clearly marked with a longer estimate at checkout.

A tracking update is available any time from My Orders once you're signed in. Shipping is free above a threshold shown at checkout, and a flat fee otherwise.

Return Policy

If a saree arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, let us know within 3 days of delivery with photos, and we'll arrange a replacement or refund.

Because of the handwoven, made-to-order nature of many pieces, we're unable to accept returns for a change of mind once an order has shipped. Cash on Delivery refunds are processed to your bank account; prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method.

Care Instructions

Dry clean only — Banarasi silk and zari work should never be machine washed or wrung. Store folded in a breathable muslin cloth, away from direct sunlight, and refold along different lines every few months to prevent permanent creasing.

Iron on the reverse side, on a low-silk setting, with a thin cloth between the iron and the fabric to protect zari (metallic) threads.

Size Guide

A standard saree is 5.5–6.5 metres long and 42–48 inches wide — it's a one-size, draped garment rather than a fitted size. The included (or separately ordered) blouse piece is typically 0.8–1 metre of matching or contrast fabric, unstitched, so it can be tailored to your exact measurements.

For a first-time buyer, we recommend a full 6.3m length for the most flexibility with draping styles.

Privacy Policy

We collect only what's needed to process your order and account: name, email, phone, and delivery address. Payment details are handled directly by our payment processor (Razorpay) — we never see or store your card or UPI details.

We don't sell your data to third parties. You can request account deletion or a copy of your data at any time by writing to us using the details on our Contact page.

Terms & Conditions

Product colours may vary slightly from photographs due to screen settings and the natural variation of handwoven silk. Prices and availability are subject to change without notice; an order already placed is honoured at its confirmed price.
